# Bug bounty rewards

Rewards are based on the severity and impact of the reported vulnerability.

| Severity | Impact | Reward options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Critical** | Most severe impact | 1 year of Discord Nitro Full, or $99 in premium AI credits (valid for 1 year) |
| **High** | High impact | 1 year of Discord Nitro Basic, or $29 in premium AI credits (valid for 1 year) |
| **Medium** | Medium impact | 1 month of Discord Nitro Full, or 2 months of Discord Nitro Basic, or $10 in premium AI credits (valid for 1 year) |
| **Low** | Low impact | 1 month of Discord Nitro Basic, or $5 in AI credits (valid for 1 year) |
| **Bugfix** | No security implications | A signed thank you card (PDF format), or $1 in premium AI credits (valid for 28 days) |

AI credits for bugfixes are issued at our discretion.
