## Overview

332 welcomes responsible security research from the community. This program is intended to encourage the discovery and responsible disclosure of security vulnerabilities in our online services.

## Scope

The following systems are in scope:

- app.order332.com (primary) and its associated assets
- *.order332.com
- Mercer bot

The following are explicitly out of scope:

- ctf.order332.com
- Telos bot

## Allowed testing

Testing must be limited to non-destructive activities. Researchers may test for vulnerabilities, but may not cause service disruption, data loss, account takeover, unauthorized access to sensitive data, or any other harmful impact.

Only non-destructive attacks against infrastructure are permitted.

## Eligibility

Only members of 332 are automatically eligible for rewards. Outsiders may be considered on a case-by-case basis at 332's discretion.

## Reporting process

To report a vulnerability:

- Members of 332 should report the issue in the server or via direct message.
- Outsiders should send a message to security@order332.com. Please note that this email is currently not monitored at the time of writing, pending infrastructure readiness.

Please include sufficient detail to reproduce the issue and describe the impact clearly.

## Disclosure policy

Public disclosure outside the Discord server is not permitted. Researchers should not publicly share details of a vulnerability until it has been handled appropriately by 332.

## Legal and ethical expectations

Researchers must act in good faith, avoid unnecessary harm, and comply with applicable laws and platform terms. Any activity that causes damage, data exposure, or service interruption is not permitted.

## Disclaimer

332 does not assume any legal liability for any actions taken by researchers in the course of security testing, except where prohibited by applicable law. Researchers are solely responsible for ensuring that their activities comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and terms of service.

## Third-party services

Some services, infrastructure, and platforms used by 332 may be provided by third-party hosting providers or other external services. Security issues affecting those third-party systems are subject to the policies and procedures of those providers. If a vulnerability is found in a third-party platform, it should be reported through that provider's own bug bounty program, security team, or appropriate disclosure channel, rather than through 332's program.
